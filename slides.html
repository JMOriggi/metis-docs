How researchers work today — and what's still missing

Today's workflow

What's still missing

Brainstorm Scope the question with peers or a general-purpose LLM (ChatGPT, Claude, OpenEvidence) to map terms and adjacent topics.
PubMed access is disconnected from AI tools. Searching the corpus and reasoning with an LLM happen in different windows; nothing flows between them.
Review papers Read, annotate, and cross-reference one paper at a time, often through PubMed and PDFs.
No quick way to build a paper context. Assembling a focused bag of papers an LLM can reason over still takes manual copy-paste and prompt wrangling.
Systematic review Run formal screening with external LLMs or tools like ASReview to triage candidates.
Tracking new papers stays manual. Staying current on a topic is an art — alerts, feeds, and ad-hoc searches that no assistant follows for you.
Researchers still juggle multiple disconnected tools to brainstorm, screen, and review.

How Metis helps

One tool that wraps the whole workflow — and keeps the researcher in charge

  • All-in-one workspace. Search, brainstorming, screening, and citation walks live in one place. No more juggling tabs between PubMed, ChatGPT, and ASReview.
  • You stay in control. The assistant helps you curate the collection of papers needed for your review/research goals. The tool helps, it never decides for you.
  • Local expert researcher LLMs. We run our own researcher-tuned models in-house, no third-party providers, no data leaving your infrastructure.
  • Reliable answers. The assistant only sees what you put in front of it, so citations are real and traceable instead of plausible-sounding fabrications.
